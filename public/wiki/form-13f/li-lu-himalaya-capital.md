## Overview
A deep-dive tutorial into the philosophy, portfolio architecture, and multi-decade compounding machine built by Li Lu, one of the greatest contemporary value investors and the founder of Himalaya Capital Management.

## The Crucible of Character
Li Lu's psychological resilience, required for extreme portfolio concentration, was forged through profound personal trials.
- A survivor of the Tangshan earthquake and a prominent student leader during the 1989 Tiananmen Square protests, he escaped to the U.S. as a political refugee.
- At Columbia University, he achieved an unprecedented academic feat: graduating simultaneously with a B.A. in Economics, a J.D. in Law, and an M.B.A.
- **The Buffett Epiphany:** While at Columbia, hearing Warren Buffett lecture completely rewired his worldview, introducing him to the rational, moral framework of compounding capital.

## Epistemology of Capital Allocation
Li Lu's framework rests on four absolute pillars:
1. **The Ownership Mindset:** Stocks are fractional ownership stakes in real businesses, not tradable paper.
2. **Mr. Market:** The market exists to serve you with continuous pricing, not to dictate intrinsic value.
3. **Margin of Safety:** Buy at a steep discount to conservatively calculated intrinsic value to absorb inevitable forecasting errors.
4. **Circle of Competence:** Only invest where you possess absolute, granular understanding that exceeds even the company's own management.

## The Munger Connection
Charlie Munger famously entrusted $88 million of his family's personal fortune to Li Lu in 2004—the *only* outside manager Munger ever utilized.
- Li Lu was instrumental in Berkshire Hathaway's legendary 2008 investment in **BYD (Build Your Dreams)**. The $232M stake eventually appreciated more than thirtyfold as BYD became the world's largest EV manufacturer.

## Q4 2025 Form 13F Architecture
Himalaya Capital's public U.S. filings (roughly $3.5B AUM) reveal extreme conviction and concentration:
- **Tech & Finance Monopolies:** The portfolio is massively anchored by **Alphabet (GOOG/GOOGL)** (~43.8%) and **Bank of America (BAC)** (~16.1%).
- **The PDD Thesis:** A contrarian ~14.6% allocation to Pinduoduo (PDD). Li Lu isolated PDD as a superior structural compounder delivering extreme value to price-sensitive consumers, looking past Western regulatory fears.

## Coattailing Hazards
Why retail investors frequently lose money trying to clone Li Lu's 13F filings:
- **Informational Latency:** 13F filings have a 45-day delay; retail investors act on decayed intelligence.
- **Incomplete Mosaic:** The SEC 13F only mandates disclosure of U.S.-listed equities. Massive non-U.S. deployments (e.g., Asian financials) are hidden, distorting the perception of Himalaya's true risk exposure.
- **Psychological Asymmetry:** You can borrow a ticker symbol, but you cannot borrow Li Lu's deep-seated conviction when the stock suffers a 50% drawdown.
