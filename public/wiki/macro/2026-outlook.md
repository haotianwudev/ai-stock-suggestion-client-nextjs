## Overview
As the global economy exits the post-pandemic recovery phase, 2026 is characterized by a "Great Normalization" where slowing growth meets sticky inflation, testing the soft-landing narrative.

## The Macro Landscape
2026 faces structural supply constraints keeping prices elevated even as demand softens.
- **Stagflation Risk** — Wage-price persistence in the service sector and energy transition costs (greenflation) create a floor under core inflation.
- **Tariffs & Trade** — Renewed protectionism, with baseline tariffs becoming standard geopolitical leverage, heavily impacting global logistics and tech hardware.
- **Rate Cut Trajectory** — The Federal Reserve is expected to pause in Q1 to assess inflation, begin cuts in Q2 to support labor, and stabilize around a new neutral rate of 3.75% by Q4.

## Sector Watch: The AI Correction
The artificial intelligence sector moves from "hype" to "ROI," entering a period of rationalization.
- **Hardware Overcapacity** — A projected 15-20% correction in pure-play hardware stocks due to chip inventory buildup and margin compression.
- **Gold Upside** — Strong forecast of $4,500-$5,000/oz driven by geopolitical risks, central bank buying, and dollar debasement.
- **Small Cap Value** — Liquidity rotation away from mega-cap tech into undervalued industrial and healthcare small caps.

## Investment Playbook
A defensive strategy prioritizing downside protection while targeting specific growth pockets.
- **Fixed Income (40%)** — Lock in duration with 5-7 year Treasuries and high-grade corporate credit as the yield curve normalizes.
- **Quality Equities (35%)** — Focus on companies with low debt, high free cash flow, and dividend growth (Consumer Staples, Healthcare, Defense).
- **Alternatives & Cash (25%)** — Maintain 10% in Gold and 15% in short-term T-Bills/cash to deploy during volatility spikes.
