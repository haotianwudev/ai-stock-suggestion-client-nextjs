## Overview
Structured notes are senior, unsecured debt obligations linked to market performance. They are hybrid instruments created by combining a zero-coupon bond with a derivative package, offering non-linear, defined outcomes (e.g., protected downside with capped upside).

## The Two Building Blocks
1. **Zero-Coupon Bond (ZCB):** Purchased at a discount, it accretes back to par value by maturity. This component sources the "Principal Protection" (subject to the issuer's credit risk).
2. **Derivative Package:** The discount from the ZCB is used to buy call and put options. This engine creates the specific "Payoff Formula" (e.g., upside participation, downside buffers, or yield).

## The Taxonomy of Customization
- **Protection Notes:** Prioritize capital preservation with full or partial protection against declines. Often feature capped upside.
- **Growth Notes:** Designed for enhanced or leveraged participation in the underlying asset's positive performance, usually in exchange for contingent protection or capped upside.
- **Yield Notes:** Aim to generate enhanced yield or regular coupons higher than comparable fixed-income by selling options (assuming downside risk).

## Advanced Structures
- **Autocallable Notes:** Feature pre-set observation dates. If the underlier is at or above the autocall level, the note is redeemed early with a premium, creating reinvestment risk.
- **Reverse Convertibles (RCN):** High-coupon products where the investor implicitly sells a put option. If a "knock-in" barrier is breached, the investor may suffer significant principal loss or receive depreciated shares (physical delivery).

## The Lehman Lesson (Credit Risk)
A structured note is ultimately an **unsecured promise to pay**. In 2008, when Lehman Brothers (a top issuer of "Principal Protected Notes") filed for bankruptcy, investors suffered catastrophic losses. Any promise of "100% Protection" is only as good as the bank's balance sheet.

## Implementation & Taxation
- **Portfolio Role:** Notes can serve as a Core Holding to reduce volatility or a Tactical Play for a specific sector view. They act as a behavioral anchor by preventing panic-selling during corrections.
- **Taxation:** Gains on many notes are taxed at ordinary income rates, and phantom income (OID) may require yearly tax payments before maturity. They are best held in tax-sheltered accounts (IRAs/401ks).
