## Overview

Stanley Druckenmiller's Duquesne Family Office 13F for Q2 2025 shows a decisive pivot to offense: total reported market value surged from $3.06B to $4.07B (+33%), holdings expanded from 52 to 69 positions, and quarterly turnover hit 60.92%. The portfolio reflects three converging convictions — a bet on the foundational AI value chain, broad bullishness on the U.S. economy (including small caps), and high-stakes, idiosyncratic healthcare bets.

## Key Concepts

- **Top-down macro framework** — Druckenmiller starts with a global economic view (rates, currencies, growth cycles) to identify attractive markets and sectors before selecting individual stocks.
- **Forward-looking conviction** — famously invests based on a visualized scenario roughly 18 months out, aiming to front-run major shifts before they're priced in.
- **Concentration and aggression** — when conviction is high, he &ldquo;goes for the jugular,&rdquo; making large, concentrated bets rather than diversifying broadly. Top 10 holdings represented 50.54% of the Q2 portfolio.
- **Capital preservation** — despite the aggressive positioning, the discipline that has defined his career (including 30 years at Duquesne Capital with no down years) is cutting losses quickly and unemotionally when a thesis is wrong.

## Anatomy of the Reshuffle

- **New cornerstones** — Entegris (AI infrastructure &ldquo;picks & shovels&rdquo;), Microsoft (AI software/cloud), new call options on the Russell 2000 (IWM, small-cap bullishness), and Citigroup (rotation into global banks).
- **Increased stakes** — Insmed (+64.6%, ahead of an FDA decision), Taiwan Semiconductor (+27.8%, reinforcing the AI foundry thesis), Roku (+123%, an aggressive streaming bet).
- **Complete exits** — Amazon (capital redeployed from mega-cap), Capital One (shift from consumer to global banks), SpringWorks Therapeutics (strategic biotech exit).
- **Major reductions** — Coupang (-55.9%, reduced emerging-markets exposure), Coherent (-47.9%, profit-taking), Philip Morris (-26.2%, trimming a defensive stalwart).

## Three Dominant Macro Themes

1. **The AI value chain bet** — rather than chasing AI application-layer hype, the new positions (Microsoft, Entegris, Taiwan Semiconductor) target the foundational, capital-intensive layers of the AI buildout — a thesis argued to be more durable than picking a single application winner.
2. **Bullish on America** — new SPY and IWM call options signal confidence not just in mega-cap leadership but in a broadening rally that lifts smaller, domestically-focused companies too.
3. **High-stakes healthcare** — the portfolio's core remains anchored in idiosyncratic, event-driven healthcare bets uncorrelated to the macro cycle, exemplified by the large pre-approval increase in Insmed ahead of its FDA decision.

## Key Holdings Deep Dive

- **Natera (NTRA)** — #1 holding at $521.2M; a long-term play on the cell-free DNA testing leader, with a minor 9.3% trim read as rebalancing after appreciation, not a thesis change.
- **Insmed (INSM)** — stake increased 64.6% just before the FDA approved BRINSUPRI (Aug 12, 2025) for a rare lung disease, validating a classic &ldquo;go for the jugular&rdquo; pre-catalyst trade.
- **Entegris (ENTG)** — largest new common-stock position; a &ldquo;picks and shovels&rdquo; bet on ultra-pure semiconductor materials that profits from rising chip complexity regardless of which chip designer wins.
- **Microsoft (MSFT)** — new mega-cap anchor betting on Azure, Copilot, and the OpenAI partnership, backed by an $80B FY25 AI capex cycle.
- **Taiwan Semiconductor (TSM)** — increased 27.8% to a top-5 holding; a bet on the quasi-monopolistic foundry behind Nvidia and Apple's most advanced chips, reinforced by blowout Q2 earnings and raised 30% revenue growth guidance.

## Key Takeaways

- The scale of the shift is the signal itself: a 33% jump in reported value combined with 60.92% turnover in a single quarter is a much stronger conviction statement than any individual position.
- The AI thesis expressed here deliberately avoids the application layer — every new AI-related position (Microsoft, Entegris, TSM) sits in infrastructure or foundational supply chain, a durability bet rather than a bet on which AI product wins.
- Small-cap call options (IWM) alongside mega-cap AI bets show this isn't a narrow AI trade — it's a broad-based wager that the U.S. economic cycle itself is durable and broadening, not narrowly concentrated in a handful of tech names.
