## Overview

Appaloosa Management's Q2 2026 13F shows total assets surging from $5.93B to $7.47B, a quarter of aggressive de-risking after a +32.0% H1 2026 gross return. David Tepper harvested gains from cyclical memory-chip pure-plays, rotated into mega-cap platform monopolies, and layered on a large synthetic macro hedge via Apple put options — all against a macro backdrop of disinflation colliding with labor-market softening.

## Key Concepts

- **ASICs (Application-Specific Integrated Circuits)** — Customized microchips designed for a specific application, such as AI training, rather than general-purpose use.
- **HBM (High-Bandwidth Memory)** — A specialized, high-performance RAM interface widely used in data centers for AI processing; the demand driver behind the Q1 memory-chip thesis.
- **PPAs (Power Purchase Agreements)** — Long-term contracts to supply electricity, crucial for data centers securing baseload power from producers like Vistra and NRG Energy.
- **ADRs (American Depositary Receipts)** — Certificates issued by a U.S. bank representing shares in a foreign stock, traded on U.S. exchanges; the vehicle for Appaloosa's Chinese-equity exits.

## Portfolio Rotation (Q1 → Q2 2026)

**Top holdings (June 30, 2026):** AMZN (15.95%, +15.74% Q2), MU (15.06%, -41.44% Q2), TSM (10.55%, +24.29%), GOOG (8.75%, +6.77%), UBER (7.43%, +21.50%).

**Full exits:** SanDisk (SNDK, was 3.01%), Corning (GLW, was 2.59%), PDD Holdings (was 1.55%), Lyft (was 0.61% — consolidated entirely into Uber).

## The AI Trade Maturation

- **Phase 1 — Memory & Hardware Cyclicality:** Pure-play memory providers (MU, SNDK) rode explosive HBM demand; liquidated as capacity expansion signaled an approaching supply glut.
- **Phase 2 — Platform Scale & Custom Silicon:** Rotation into hyperscalers and custom chip designers (AMZN, GOOG, and a new AVGO position at 0.76%) for vertical integration and durable free-cash-flow protection.
- **Infrastructure and power bets:** New positions in CoreWeave (CRWV, 1.44%, GPU-as-a-Service) and SpaceX (SPCX, 0.51%), plus retained Vistra (4.70%) and NRG Energy (3.44%) stakes as bets on grid power as the ultimate AI bottleneck.

## The De-Risking Trigger

$$
\text{Valuation Premium} = \frac{\text{Market Price}}{\text{Intrinsic Value}} - 1
$$

**Worked example (Micron, illustrative):** Market Price $1,154.00 vs. Intrinsic Value ≈ $686.90 gives a Valuation Premium of 68.0% — well past the ~50% threshold the fund's model uses to trigger a sell-into-strength exit, which drove the Micron harvest.

## Tactical Whipsaws

Appaloosa fully exited airlines (AAL, DAL, UAL) in Q1 2026, then reversed hard in Q2: a new 7.5M-share AAL position (1.81% weight) bought under $15/share on a compressed forward P/E and $2B FCF pipeline, plus 800k shares of Boeing (BA) bought at peak operational despair.

## Hedging the Heights: The Apple Put Strategy

- **Position:** 835,000 AAPL put contracts.
- **Weight:** 3.13% of the portfolio (~$241.6M equivalent value).
- **Function:** A synthetic macro hedge — because AAPL anchors the S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100, these puts provide non-linear payoff buffering against broad index drawdowns or hawkish rate shocks, insulating the fund's heavy mega-cap tech concentration (AMZN, GOOG, META, TSM, NVDA).

## Key Takeaways

- **Defensive rotation:** Assets grew from $5.93B to $7.47B while capital shifted from high-beta semiconductor momentum into mega-cap technology monopolies.
- **AI maturation:** Profit-taking on memory pure-plays in favor of scalable platform compute.
- **Macro hedging:** A 3.13% AAPL put weighting to insulate against index-wide tech multiple compression.
- **Contrarian agility:** Full Q1 exits from airlines/aerospace followed by aggressive Q2 distressed re-entries in the same names.
- **Structural China de-risking:** Complete exit from PDD, JD.com, and KWEB, with BABA trimmed 42.28% to a 2.57% weight.
