## Overview

Duquesne Family Office LLC's Q3 2026 Form 13F-HR (CIK `0001536411`, Accession No. `0001536411-26-000011`, filed November 16, 2026 for the quarter ended September 30, 2026) discloses a structural overhaul across **\$4.47 billion** (\$4,470,867k) in reportable U.S.-listed equity and call-option holdings. Line items compressed from **95** in Q2 2026 to **65** across **60 distinct issuers** (42 exits, 16 new initiations, 16 additions, and 15 trims).

Disaggregating common shares from option notional exposure reveals that **72.25% (\$534.64M of the \$739.99M headline book decline)** came from extinguishing macro index and mega-cap call options (-62.41% QoQ), while ex-options cash equity stayed essentially stable (\$4,148,873k, -4.72% QoQ). Portfolio concentration more than doubled, with Top 10 combined issuers reaching **70.66% (\$3,158.9M)** and the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (**HHI**) rising from `0.049` to `0.1110`.

## Key Concepts

- **Form 13F-HR** — Mandatory quarterly SEC filing disclosing only long U.S.-listed equities, ADRs, and exchange-traded options as of quarter-end (with a 45-day reporting lag), omitting short sales, Treasury futures, currency swaps, private venture stakes, and subsequent trades.
- **Call Option Notional Value** — Form 13F requires institutional managers to report exchange-traded options at the notional market value of the underlying shares controlled rather than the cash premium spent.
- **Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI)** — Quantitative measure of portfolio concentration across combined issuer weights, which more than doubled in Duquesne's book from `0.049` in Q2 2026 to `0.1110` in Q3 2026.
- **HAMR (Heat-Assisted Magnetic Recording)** — Seagate's Mozaic platform (targeting 44TB and 50TB drives, with Mozaic 4+ qualified at the two largest cloud service providers) underpinning its cost-per-terabyte moat in AI data-center storage.
- **Fiscal Dominance** — A macroeconomic regime where massive peacetime government deficits (~6% of GDP at \$40T national debt) collide with monetary policy, and Treasury buybacks of long bonds funded by short-term T-bills act as disguised quantitative easing that fuels inflation.
- **Forward Guidance** — Central bank communication providing future rate-path promises or an automatic equity "put" — explicitly dropped by Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh to remove rate-cut optionality.
- **Molecular Residual Disease (MRD)** — Clinical oncology testing led by Natera's cell-free DNA (cfDNA) *Signatera* assay to detect microscopic cancer remnants across colorectal, breast, and additional tumor indications.
- **PURR / HYPE** — Hyperliquid Strategies Inc (`PURR`) is a Nasdaq-listed digital-asset treasury company accumulating ~38.9M `HYPE` tokens, the native asset of the Hyperliquid Layer-1 blockchain (70% of decentralized perpetual futures volume).

## Formulas

### 1. Disaggregating Total Book Decline vs. Option Notional

$$
\Delta \text{Book}_{\text{Total}} = \Delta \text{Equity}_{\text{Ex-Opt}} + \Delta \text{Call}_{\text{Notional}}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta \text{Book}_{\text{Total}} &= \$4{,}470{,}867\text{k} - \$5{,}210{,}856\text{k} = -\$739{,}989\text{k}\;(-14.20\%) \\
\Delta \text{Call}_{\text{Notional}} &= \$321{,}994\text{k} - \$856{,}636\text{k} = -\$534{,}642\text{k}\;(-62.41\%) \\
\Delta \text{Equity}_{\text{Ex-Opt}} &= \$4{,}148{,}873\text{k} - \$4{,}354{,}220\text{k} = -\$205{,}347\text{k}\;(-4.72\%)
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus **72.25%** (\$534.64M / \$739.99M) of the headline Q3 reduction came from liquidating \$514.9M in macro ETF and mega-cap calls (`RSP`, `EWZ`, `IWM`, `SPY`, `META`) rather than withdrawing cash equity.

### 2. Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) Concentration

$$
\text{HHI} = \sum_{i=1}^{N} w_i^2
$$

Where $w_i$ is the combined portfolio weight of issuer $i$ across all $N = 60$ unique issuers. Anchor holding Natera alone contributes:

$$
w_{\text{NTRA}} = \frac{\$1{,}306{,}335\text{k}}{\$4{,}470{,}867\text{k}} = 29.22\%, \qquad w_{\text{NTRA}}^2 = 0.0854
$$

Total portfolio HHI jumped from `0.049` (Q2) to **`0.1110`** (Q3).

### 3. Natera (NTRA) Post-Earnings Q4 Mark-to-Market

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta \text{MV}_{\text{NTRA}} &= \text{Shares} \times (P_{\text{Nov 16}} - P_{\text{Sep 30}}) \\
&= 3{,}160{,}666 \times (\$298.77 - \$413.31) = -\$362.03\text{M}\;(-27.71\%)
\end{aligned}
$$

Despite the -\$362.03M Q4 pullback (\$1,306.34M to \$944.31M), the stake remains **+142.3%** (+\$175.47/sh) above Duquesne's ~\$123.30 estimated average cost basis.

## Why Druckenmiller Made the Q3 Changes

### 1. AI Value-Chain Rotation: Foundries & Hyperscalers Out, Storage & Monetizers In

- **Exits & Trims:** Slashed Taiwan Semiconductor (`TSM`, -94.45% / -556,980 sh, locking in +73.97% over ~\$229.14 basis), cut `STM` -47.94%, and fully exited `AMD` (\$42.3M), SanDisk (`SNDK`, \$79.4M after +257.9% peak gain), `LRCX`, `ARM`, and Alphabet (`GOOGL`, \$120.2M exited as AI capex and a \$17B Crusoe Wyoming takeover pushed Q3 FCF negative).
- **Accumulation:** Nearly doubled enterprise HDD storage to \$442.4M (9.90% of book) via Seagate (`STX` equity +180.16% to \$315.25M + \$90.39M calls) and new Western Digital (`WDC`, \$36.79M). Surged Unity (`U`, +435.53% to \$161.94M), initiated AppLovin (`APP`, \$31.79M), Palantir (`PLTR`, \$14.98M), `TTMI` (\$27.21M), `AMZN` equity (+42.65%), and newly public SpaceX (`SPCX`, \$30.49M / 0.68%).
- **Fundamental Sizing Contrast:** `STX` (9.07% combined) generated \$3.11B FQ3 revenue, record 47.0% gross margin, \$953M FCF, and 0.53x PEG, whereas `SPCX` (0.68% capped starter) carries -\$4.94B FY25 consolidated net loss and ~115x sales after its Feb 2026 xAI merger and June 12 Nasdaq IPO.

### 2. Macro & Fiscal Dominance: "Let the Bond Market Speak"

- **Treasury vs. Fed Collision:** As U.S. debt crossed \$40T with peacetime deficits near 6% of GDP, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent doubled long-bond buybacks to \$4B on Aug 19. In an Aug 24 *WSJ* op-ed, Druckenmiller rebuked buybacks funded by T-bills as disguised Treasury QE (*"If the 30-year must trade at 5.5% to clear, that isn't a crisis. It is an invoice."*). Meanwhile, Fed Chair Kevin Warsh dropped forward guidance and hiked +25 bps to 3.75%–4.00% on Sept 16.
- **Portfolio Repositioning:** Liquidated 100% of macro index calls (`RSP`, `EWZ`, `IWM`, `SPY` — \$486.9M), exited airlines (`UAL` \$108.1M, `DAL` \$56.3M) amid rising diesel costs, trimmed infrastructure (`CRH` -71.21%, `FLR` -26.39%), and deployed **\$138.04M (3.09%)** into gold miners (`NEM`, `B`, `AEM`, `SKE`) near \$3,850–\$3,900/oz spot lows.

### 3. Idiosyncratic Secular Alpha Sleeves

- **Precision Oncology & Biotech (\$1,853.28M / 41.45% across 20 lines):** Anchored by Natera (`NTRA`, 29.22%), alongside `INSM` (\$187.49M combined), `RVMD` (+9.18% to \$89.55M), `NAMS` (+9.35% to \$81.65M), `RYTM` (+32.55%), `BEAM` (+355.53%), and re-initiated `HUM` (\$24.89M).
- **LatAm & Asian E-Commerce (\$598.04M / 13.38%):** Added `YPF` (+12.84% to \$177.56M) and `JBS` (+114.39%), shifted Brazil (`EWZ`, \$125.81M) from calls to shares, trimmed `TBBB` (-43.23%), and boosted `SE` (+44.81% to \$155.07M).
- **U.S. Bedding & Manufactured Housing (\$132.75M / 2.97%):** Initiated Somnigroup (`SGI`, \$61.71M), added `SKY` (+45.91%), and held `CVCO` and `RKT`.
- **On-Chain Perpetuals & Digital Assets (\$94.01M / 2.10%):** Held `PURR` (\$36.09M, ~38.9M `HYPE` tokens), `BTDR` (\$39.67M), and `RIOT` (+19.98% to \$18.25M), followed by a post-quarter private co-investment with `ICE` in `trade[XYZ]` on Oct 29, 2026.

## Q4 Market Echoes (Through Nov 17, 2026)

- **Rates & Fed Trajectory:** After an Oct 27–28 FOMC hold (10-2 vote with two hawkish dissents), 3.4% Q3 GDP, 4.1% unemployment, and hot Nov 10 CPI pushed 10-year Treasury yields past **5.15%** (2Y at 4.88%) and Dec 8–9 FOMC hike odds to **92%** (+25 bps) / **8%** (+50 bps).
- **Protective Value of Q3 Exits:** Broad tech sell-offs hit Oct 30, Nov 2, and Nov 5 (`SPX` -4.4% single day). Exited/avoided names suffered steep drawdowns: `SNDK` (-66.7% off late-June peak), `GOOGL` (~30% off May peak; -18% post-Sept 30), `AMD` (-22% off Oct 6), and `NVDA` (~30% off \$243.37 Oct 6 high).
- **Active Holdings Stress-Test:** `NTRA` fell -27.7% (\$413.31 to \$298.77 on Nov 16) on higher R&D (\$242.4M) and GAAP EPS miss (-\$0.42 vs -\$0.26 est) despite beating Q3 revenue (\$794.0M, +34.1% YoY) and raising FY26 guidance (\$3.005B–\$3.035B). `STX` pulled back to ~\$809 (-11.56% window), `SPCX` slipped to \$135.00–\$139.98 post-midterms, and `PURR` dropped ~42% from late Sept to \$8.16–\$8.70 amid \$1.52B weekly crypto ETF outflows even as `HYPE` held \$66.00–\$66.90.

## Key Takeaways

- **Migrate dynamically along the AI S-curve:** Rotate out of crowded front-end compute and FCF-burning hyperscalers into contracted physical bottlenecks (`STX`/`WDC` HAMR storage) and current software monetizers (`U`, `APP`, `PLTR`).
- **Enforce zero-ego FCF discipline:** With 72% LTM turnover and sub-7-month average top-20 holding periods, exit immediately when capex turns FCF negative (`GOOGL`) regardless of entry basis.
- **Strip out index beta when the Fed Put fades:** Under a Warsh Fed dropping forward guidance, cut leveraged index calls (`SPY`, `IWM`, `RSP`) in favor of non-correlated clinical alpha (`NTRA`, `RVMD`) and hard-asset hedges (`NEM`, `B`, `AEM`).
- **Barbell conviction with starter caps:** Let structural winners compound (`NTRA` at 29.2%) while capping high-multiple, loss-making IPOs (`SPCX` at 0.68%).
- **Account for the 45-day lag and 13F blind spots:** Form 13F omits short Treasury bets and private deals (`trade[XYZ]`), and Q4 pullbacks (`NTRA` at \$298.77 vs \$413.31) can offer better entries than the Sept 30 filing mark.
